Connect a new client store

About 5 minutes. Shopify needs one small app per client store, made in your Dev Dashboard.

  1. Ask the client for their store's .myshopify.com address (in their Shopify admin: Settings → Domains).
  2. Open dev.shopify.com/dashboard → Create app. Name it, for example "SEO for client name".
  3. In the app's new version, fill in:
    App URL
    Redirect URL
    Scopes
    Turn off "Embed app in Shopify admin", then click Release.
  4. Go to Distribution → Custom distribution → enter the client's .myshopify.com address. (Shopify locks this app to that one store.)
  5. From the app's Settings, copy the Client ID and Client secret into the form below and click Add store.
  6. Click Connect next to the store and approve. Or send the client the install link from the Distribution page, and they approve it themselves.
⚠ This website runs on your own computer (127.0.0.1), so the last step only works when you click Connect on this computer. You need access to the client's store for that (they can add you as a staff member or collaborator). Once the site is online, set PUBLIC_URL in the settings, and clients can approve from anywhere.
